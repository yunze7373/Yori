## Typography & Font Assets

All fonts utilized in Yori are self-hosted locally under `public/fonts/` (bundled into `dist/fonts/` in shipping builds). Yori maintains zero runtime dependencies on external font delivery networks (e.g. Google Fonts CDN), preserving user privacy and offline operability.
